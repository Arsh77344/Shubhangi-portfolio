Hello, I’m Shubhangi

Eight years, two countries, and what looks like a few careers but is really one story.

I started in engineering and consulting at Infosys, opens in a new tab, where I learned how global companies actually work. Then I did my MSc in marketing at the University of Leeds, opens in a new tab, and today I build international partnerships and growth for a university.

The thread through all of it is that I care less about the technology or the title, and more about how things get built across borders, often from nothing.

Today I’m based in India, working remotely as Recruitment Manager, South Asia, for Luminate Education Group, opens in a new tab.

How I work

Five things you can count on.

The habits that show up in every role, whether it’s a pipeline review or a food drive.

  • I build what’s missing

    When the market, the network or the process doesn’t exist yet, that’s where I start.

  • Communication is a tool

    Clear updates, honest reports and the right message for the right person keep work moving.

  • People first, then systems

    Partners and teams have to trust the plan before any dashboard or CRM will help.

  • The data has to be right

    I check the numbers before they leave my desk. Clean data is a commercial asset.

  • I lead without a title

    From 2,000 volunteers to cross-border teams, I get people moving in the same direction.
